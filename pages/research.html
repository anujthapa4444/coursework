Research and Design Comparison

We researched navigation, page bodies, product layouts, and cart interactions from real websites. For the home page, we used a reference nav bar style; for every page we adapted body layout ideas to keep the site consistent and easy to use.

The research covers each page part: the header/nav bar, the main body section, and the product page cart section, showing where each design idea came from.

Research by Page Component

Navigation Bar

We researched clear top navigation from site homepages like REI and other retail/eco sites. We chose a sticky navbar with a bold brand, simple links, and a visible active state for easy page switching.

Home Page Body

The home page body is based on hero sections and summary card layouts from similar lifestyle and gardening sites. It uses a large headline, short supporting text, and quick links to the products, blog, and research pages for a friendly first impression.

Products Page and Cart Section

The products page layout borrows from e-commerce pages such as Amazon with product cards, price labels, and a category filter. The cart drawer is modeled after side cart patterns seen on shopping sites, with a clear item list, totals, and a simple checkout-style action.

This part shows comparisons for each page, with pictures, similarity scores, and check times.
